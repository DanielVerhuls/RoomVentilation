Things to try

  • max |div u| is mass conservation after the pressure projection; it should stay near 1e-14.
  • Lower the viscosity: the inlet jet starts to flap and shed vortices. At the real value for air, what you see is mostly the scheme's own numerical diffusion.
  • Compare cell sizes 0.1 and 0.025: the finer grid resolves sharper jets but runs slower.
  • Pressure: the jet hitting a wall builds up pressure (stagnation), vortex cores are low-pressure spots, and the outlet is the 0 Pa reference.
